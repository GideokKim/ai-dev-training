## Exact Image Prompt

Use case: productivity-visual.
Asset type: two small role pictograms in a transparent horizontal 2:1 sprite, for a professional Korean engineering training PowerPoint.
Primary request: exactly two separate, equally sized symbols centered in equal left and right halves. LEFT: a neutral human developer bust, clearly a person, simple head and shoulders, dark-slate outline, restrained pale gray-blue fill. RIGHT: an AI agent symbol, an abstract small rounded-square machine head with two simple sensor dots and a subtle circuit-node motif, dark-slate outline with muted teal accents. It must read as software assistance, not as a real human.
Style: crisp modern editorial pictograms, professional, calm, consistent stroke thickness and visual weight. Match a Light Engineering Workbench palette: dark slate #17212B, muted teal #0B6172, pale gray-blue. Clean flat line illustration, minimal shading, no gradients, no heavy shadow.
Composition: landscape exactly 2:1. Equal square halves; one centered pictogram per half, each using roughly 70 percent of its square. At least 12 percent clear padding inside each half. Neither symbol may cross the vertical center line. No connecting arrow, no plus sign. Background fully transparent.
Text: none. No letters, words, numbers, labels, captions, logos or watermark. Editable Korean/English labels will be added in PowerPoint.
Constraints: only these two symbols, no laptop, no person holding documents, no scene, no card borders. The human symbol will be reused in both columns of a responsibility comparison. These are teaching icons, not photos, screenshots, observed product interfaces, or claims about autonomous authority.
